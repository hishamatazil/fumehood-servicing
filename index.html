Frequently asked questions.

Q: How often does my fume hood need to be serviced?

Annual certification by a qualified technician is required. Our Annual Servicing Contract also includes quarterly preventive maintenance in between certifications.

Q: What's included in a servicing visit?

Face velocity testing, inward airflow confirmation, alarm system testing, a comprehensive inspection report, and compliant documentation.

Q: Do I get proof of certification?

Yes — a certification label is affixed to the cabinet, and you'll receive a full inspection/certification report for your records and audits.
